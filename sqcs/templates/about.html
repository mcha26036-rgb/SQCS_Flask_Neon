About SQCS

A web-based quality governance platform for structured software assessments.

◫

Projects

Create, edit and track assessment projects with ownership, dates, standards and lifecycle status.

✓

Checklists

Run configurable multi-section reviews with Excellent, Need Review, Fail and N/A response states plus persistent feedback.

▤

Evidence

Upload, tag, search and download quality evidence alongside assessment work.

◈

Reports

Generate print-ready project reports with executive results and section-level performance.

⚙

Governance

Administer users, roles, templates and destructive project operations with an audit trail.

∞

Deployment

Designed for Vercel serverless deployment with Neon PostgreSQL persistence and environment-based configuration.

How the workflow works

01Create project

Define scope, owner and standard.

02Run checklist

Record evidence-driven responses.

03Review risk

Inspect section performance and failed controls.

04Report & export

Generate a report and preserve the JSON assessment record.
